# M3 Finance Design

**Status:** Approved 10 September 2026

## Scope

M3 adds wedding-scoped budget categories, budget scenarios and scenario items, committed budget items, repeatable payment terms with actual-payment fields, savings goals/contributors/contributions, authoritative financial summaries, finance activity logs, and finance summaries on the existing dashboard.

M3 excludes vendor management/selection (M4), payment-proof attachments (M6), budget custom fields (M6), exports (M7), and all other M4+ work. Completed M0–M2 behavior remains unchanged.

## Architecture

Finance remains in the Laravel/Inertia modular monolith. Laravel owns authorization, validation, state transitions, calculations, transactions, audit logging, and persistence. React receives explicit server-owned props and uses Inertia forms. Every query begins from the active wedding; owner/editor may mutate and viewers are read-only.

## Budget categories

`budget_categories` has a numeric primary key, UUID route key, direct `wedding_id`, `name`, `color`, `sort_order`, `is_active`, and timestamps. `is_active` defaults to true. Names are case-insensitively unique per wedding. Active categories appear in selectors and summaries. Referenced categories cannot be deleted; they may be deactivated, remain visible on historical records, and cannot be assigned to new items.

## Budget scenarios

`budget_scenarios` has a numeric primary key, UUID route key, direct `wedding_id`, `name`, `description`, `sort_order`, `is_active`, and timestamps. `sort_order` controls stable display order; `is_active` defaults true and supports archiving. Scenario totals are derived from active child items.

`scenario_items` has a numeric primary key, UUID route key, direct `wedding_id`, scenario/category references, optional event reference, `name`, whole-rupiah `amount`, `notes`, `sort_order`, `is_active`, and timestamps.

New weddings receive independent curated Rp10m, Rp20m, Rp30m, Rp50m, Rp75m, and Rp100m scenarios whose active allocations sum exactly to their advertised totals. Copying creates a new scenario and entirely new child rows with no source-item linkage. Applying appends independent draft budget items transactionally, never replaces or merges existing items, and repeat application requires confirmation.

## Budget items

`budget_items` has a numeric primary key, UUID route key, direct `wedding_id`, category reference, optional event reference, `name`, non-negative `estimated_amount`, nullable non-negative `final_amount`, `status`, `notes`, optional immutable scenario-name/item-name snapshots, optional application batch UUID, and timestamps.

Statuses are `draft`, `committed`, and `cancelled`. Committing requires `final_amount`. Cancelled rows remain historical and are excluded from active totals. Untouched drafts may be deleted; committed items or items with payment history must be cancelled. No vendor field is added in M3.

## Payment terms and actual payments

`payment_terms` has a numeric primary key, UUID route key, direct `wedding_id`, budget-item reference, `name`, `sort_order`, non-negative `planned_amount`, optional `due_date`, non-negative `actual_paid_amount`, optional `paid_at`, `status`, `notes`, and timestamps. There are no fixed installment columns, and one item supports at least ten terms.

Laravel derives `planned` when actual is zero, `partially_paid` when actual is positive but below planned, and `paid` when actual equals or exceeds planned. `cancelled` is explicit. Positive actual amounts require `paid_at`; zero actual clears it. A term with a positive actual amount cannot be cancelled until corrected to zero. Reordering is transactional. Payment proofs are deferred to M6.

M3 does not add a separate payment ledger: each term stores its planned amount and actual settlement (`actual_paid_amount`, `paid_at`). Multiple payments are represented as multiple terms.

## Savings

`saving_goals` has a numeric primary key, UUID route key, direct `wedding_id`, `name`, non-negative `target_amount`, optional `target_date`, `is_active`, notes, and timestamps.

`saving_contributors` has a numeric primary key, UUID route key, direct `wedding_id`, optional same-wedding membership reference, immutable display `label`, `sort_order`, `is_active`, and timestamps. The label represents either the linked member snapshot or a custom identity. A removed membership nulls the link without losing history.

`saving_contributions` has a numeric primary key, UUID route key, direct `wedding_id`, same-wedding goal/contributor references, positive `amount`, `contributed_on`, optional `source`, notes, and timestamps. Contribution deletion requires confirmation, is audited, and leaves unrelated history unchanged.

## Calculations

Laravel derives:

- scenario total from active scenario items;
- estimated total from non-cancelled budget items;
- final committed total from committed items;
- paid total from non-cancelled terms belonging to committed, non-cancelled items;
- outstanding as final committed minus paid, retaining negative overpayment;
- item/category/event/wedding breakdowns and upcoming terms;
- active savings target, collected total, gap, percentage, monthly trend, contributor breakdown, and monthly breakdown.

Browser totals are never trusted or persisted. Wedding-local dates determine monthly grouping.

## Isolation, authorization, and audit

Every finance row contains `wedding_id`. Tenant-sensitive references use composite `(wedding_id, id)` foreign keys, wedding-first indexes, and wedding-scoped uniqueness. Routes use canonical wedding UUIDs, `wedding.member`, scoped nested bindings, Form Requests, Policies, wedding-first relationship queries, and transactional Actions. Non-members receive 403, forbidden mutations receive 403, and cross-wedding nested resources resolve as 404.

Activity logs record scenario application, monetary changes, budget commitment/cancellation, actual-payment/state changes, savings-target changes, and contribution creation/update/deletion. Entries contain wedding, actor, action, subject, timestamp, and controlled before/after values—not arbitrary payloads.

## UI

- Finance overview: budget/savings summaries, overpayment warning, upcoming payments, and category/event breakdowns.
- Scenarios: list, create, rename, copy, archive/delete, child editor, apply confirmation, and repeated-application warning.
- Budget: searchable/filterable table, responsive rows, create/edit, commit/cancel, and authoritative totals.
- Budget item: summary and ordered add/edit/reorder/remove payment-term workflow.
- Savings: goals, contributors, contributions, and calculated breakdowns.
- Existing dashboard: bounded finance summary and upcoming payments.

The sidebar adds Finance. Pages include empty, loading/processing, validation, error, success, destructive-confirmation, and viewer read-only states. Text/icons accompany status colors.

## Testing

Use focused backend tests for each slice: schema/composite constraints, scenario totals/copy/application, policies, budget lifecycle, ten terms, derived payment states, calculations/overpayment, transactions, savings behavior, audit logs, dashboard integration, and three-wedding isolation. Add frontend tests only for meaningful payment editing, validation, repeated-application confirmation, and read-only behavior. Do not test static styling, trivial wrappers, or framework behavior. Run the complete backend/frontend quality gate once, after all M3 slices are complete.
